The Science of Small Groups

Why 3–5 is the conversation sweet spot.

Every group size creates a different dynamic. After hundreds of curated conversations, we've found that 3–5 is where real dialogue happens — where every person speaks, every perspective is heard, and no one drifts into passive observation.

1-on-1 Coffee

Intense

High pressure, interview-like dynamic. No variety of perspective. If the chemistry is off, there's no buffer.

3–5 People ← LinkUp

Perfect

Everyone contributes. Multiple viewpoints emerge naturally. Enough energy to sustain 90 minutes. Small enough that no one hides.

8–12 Person Meetup

Split

Table naturally fragments into 2–3 side conversations. Louder voices dominate. Introverts disengage.

20+ Person Mixer

Chaotic

Surface-level introductions on repeat. "What do you do?" becomes the default. You leave with LinkedIn connections but no real conversations.

The 24-Hour Rule

Why we share the cafe 24 hours before.

Early Enough to Plan

24 hours gives you time to check the location, plan your route, and adjust your day. No last-minute scrambling.

Precise Location Details

You receive the partner cafe name, reserved table number, and an exact Google Maps pin — delivered to your WhatsApp.

Partner Cafe Network

We work with vetted, well-lit cafes that understand our format. Tables are pre-reserved, not first-come-first-serve.

Safety by Design

Public venues in established localities. The broad area is confirmed early so you always know the neighbourhood before committing.
