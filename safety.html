Your Safety Comes First

Safety & Verification Policy

Meaningful in-person connection needs real infrastructure. Here's exactly how we keep every table safe, verified, and accountable.

Mandatory Government ID Verification

Every person who sits at a LinkUp table has been verified with a valid Government-issued ID. This is non-negotiable and applies to every attendee, every time.

We accept Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Driving Licence as valid identification. The ID is uploaded once during your first application via the Google Form.

What we check

Full legal name

Matches the name on your application

Photo identity

Valid photo on Aadhaar, PAN, or DL

Document validity

Non-expired, legible Government-issued document

We do NOT store sensitive numbers

Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, and DL numbers are not retained after verification

Data Protection Pledge

Clear commitments about how we handle your information.

Never Shared with Attendees

Other people at your table will never see your ID, your full name from the ID, or any personal identification details. They only know the first name you provide.

Never Shared with Cafes

Partner cafes receive only a table reservation under the LinkUp name. They never receive attendee IDs, phone numbers, or personal information.

Never Stored Publicly

Your ID is stored in a restricted-access system, accessible only to the verification team. It is never uploaded to public servers, social media, or shared databases.

Purpose-Limited Use

Your ID is used solely for identity verification. We do not use it for marketing, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond confirming you are who you say you are.

Partner Venue Standard

Every LinkUp cafe is personally vetted by our team. We check lighting, seating arrangement, noise levels, accessibility, and overall safety before adding any venue to our roster.

Verified Public Cafes Only

Every LinkUp meetup happens at an established, well-lit, public cafe in a known locality across Navi Mumbai. We never host at private residences, co-working spaces, or outdoor locations without proper infrastructure.

Well-lit spaces — No basements, dim corners, or enclosed private rooms.

Staffed venues — Active cafe staff present throughout the meetup.

Known localities — Kharghar, Seawoods, Belapur, Vashi, Nerul, Airoli, Panvel.

Reserved seating — Your table is pre-booked, not first-come-first-served.

Our safety promise, in plain language.

Every person at your table has been ID-verified with Aadhaar, PAN, or Driving Licence.

Your ID is never shown to other attendees, cafe partners, or third parties.

All meetups happen at vetted, well-lit, public cafes with active staff.

Group sizes are strictly 3–5 people. No solo meetups, no large mixers.

The exact cafe and table number are shared 24 hours in advance via WhatsApp.
