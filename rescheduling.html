Scheduling Policy

Scheduling, Rescheduling & Seat Fees

How we coordinate timing, handle schedule changes, and manage the reservation fee. Everything transparent, nothing hidden.

How We Select the Final Time Slot

LinkUp does not work on a first-come-first-served basis. Instead, the company selects the optimal meeting slot based on group availability overlap.

The scheduling process

1

You share broad availability windows

In the application form, you select which days and time ranges work for you (e.g., "Saturday afternoon" or "Sunday morning").

2

We find the best overlap across 3–5 people

Our team cross-references availability across all matched profiles and identifies the time slot that works for the most people in your group.

3

The company finalizes the slot

LinkUp has final discretion on timing. We aim to maximize overlap, but the final decision factors in cafe availability, travel distances, and group balance.

4

You're notified via WhatsApp

Once the slot is locked, you receive a confirmation message with the finalized day, time window, and broad locality. The exact cafe follows 24 hours before.

Rescheduling & Roll-Over Rules

Schedules change. Here's how we handle it fairly.

A

Your availability changes before confirmation

If you notify us before your table is confirmed and the seat fee is collected, we'll simply roll you over to the next compatible slot. No fee, no penalty. Let us know via WhatsApp as early as possible.

B

Your schedule clashes after confirmation

If your confirmed table's time no longer works, we'll attempt to move you to another compatible table that weekend. If no alternative exists, the seat fee is non-refundable as the cafe reservation and group coordination have already been locked.

C

A table needs rebalancing

If another attendee drops out and the table falls below 3 people, we may merge groups, defer the table to the following weekend, or cancel. If we cancel a confirmed table and cannot offer a reasonable alternative, your seat fee is fully refunded.

D

No-show on the day

If you don't show up without notifying us in advance, the seat fee is forfeited. No-shows disrupt the experience for the 3–4 other people who planned their day around the table. We take attendance seriously.

Seat Fee & Cancellation Guidelines

The seat fee is a flat, non-refundable reservation charge collected at the end of the Google Form after your table is confirmed. It exists for three reasons:

Guarantee attendance. Skin in the game dramatically reduces no-shows.

Reserve the cafe table. Partner cafes hold tables for LinkUp — the fee ensures that reservation is honoured.

Support curation. ID verification, group matching, and logistics coordination require time and care.

Refund exception

If LinkUp cancels a confirmed table and cannot offer a reasonable alternative slot, the seat fee is fully refunded. This applies only to company-side cancellations, not attendee-initiated changes.
